# AI KING Trademark Filing Guide

## Executive Summary

This document provides a practical guide for filing the AI KING trademark with appropriate trademark offices. The trademark covers a generative general intelligence system with trained AI agents, an AI orchestrator, and supporting tools.

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Trademark Name** | AI KING |
| **Mark Type** | Word Mark (or Design Mark if logo included) |
| **Primary Classes** | 9, 35, 42 |
| **Filing Basis** | Intent to Use / Use in Commerce |
| **Distinctiveness** | Suggestive to Arbitrary |

## Filing Instructions by Jurisdiction

### United States (USPTO)

#### Step 1: Prepare Application
1. **Trademark Search**: Conduct comprehensive search at USPTO.gov TESS database
2. **Mark Details**: AI KING (word mark)
3. **Owner Information**: Prepare legal entity details
4. **Goods/Services**: Use descriptions from classification sections above

#### Step 2: Filing via TEAS
- **System**: Use TEAS Plus or TEAS Standard
- **TEAS Plus**: Lower fees, stricter requirements
- **TEAS Standard**: More flexibility

#### Step 3: Required Information
```
Applicant Name: [Legal Entity Name]
Applicant Address: [Complete Address]
Applicant Entity Type: [Corporation/LLC/Individual]

Trademark: AI KING
Mark Type: Standard Characters (Word Mark)

Filing Basis: 
[ ] Use in Commerce (requires specimens)
[ ] Intent to Use (no specimens initially required)

Classes: 9, 35, 42
```

#### Step 5: Specimens of Use (if Use in Commerce)
Required specimens:
- **Class 9**: Screenshot showing software download page or actual software interface with AI KING mark
- **Class 35**: Marketing materials showing business services offered under AI KING
- **Class 42**: Website showing SaaS platform with AI KING branding

#### Step 6: Filing Fees (2024 Rates)
- TEAS Plus: $250 per class = $750 total (3 classes)
- TEAS Standard: $350 per class = $1,050 total (3 classes)

#### Step 7: Post-Filing Actions
1. Monitor application status on USPTO portal
2. Respond to Office Actions within 6 months
3. File Statement of Use if Intent to Use filing (within 6 months of Notice of Allowance, extendable)
4. File Section 8 Declaration (between 5th-6th year)
5. File Section 8 & 9 Combined (between 9th-10th year, then every 10 years)

### European Union (EUIPO)

#### Filing Process
1. **Search**: Conduct EU trademark search at euipo.europa.eu
2. **System**: Use eSearch plus for searching, eFile for application
3. **Languages**: Choose one language for filing (recommended: English)
4. **Classes**: Same Nice Classifications (9, 35, 42)

#### Fees (2024)
- Basic fee: €850 (covers one class)
- Second class: €50
- Third class: €150
- **Total**: €1,050 for three classes

#### Key Differences from US
- No "Intent to Use" - must show genuine intent
- Faster process (typically 4-6 months if no opposition)
- EU-wide protection with single registration
- Must use mark within 5 years to avoid cancellation

### United Kingdom (UKIPO)

Post-Brexit, requires separate filing:
- **Fee**: £170 per class = £510 total (3 classes)
- **Online filing**: via gov.uk/register-a-trademark
- **Process**: Similar to EU, typically 3-4 months

### International (Madrid Protocol)

For multi-country protection:
1. **Base**: File from home country (US or EU)
2. **Designate**: Select countries for protection
3. **Fees**: Basic fee + individual designation fees
4. **Advantage**: Single application, multiple countries
5. **Timing**: File international within 6 months to claim priority

#### Madrid Protocol Fees
- Basic fee: 653 CHF (individual designation)
- Per-class fees vary by country
- More economical for 3+ countries

## Filing Strategy Recommendation

### Phase 1: Initial Protection (Immediate)
1. **File in home jurisdiction** (US or EU)
   - Classes: 9, 35, 42
   - Basis: Intent to Use (US) or genuine intent (EU)
   - Priority date established

### Phase 2: International Expansion (Within 6 months)
2. **File Madrid Protocol application**
   - Claim priority from Phase 1
   - Designate: EU, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea
   - Cost-effective multi-country coverage

### Phase 3: Strategic Markets (6-12 months)
3. **Direct national filings** where needed
   - China (non-Madrid route often better)
   - India
   - Brazil
   - Other strategic markets

## Timeline Expectations

### United States
- **Filing to Registration**: 12-18 months (if no issues)
- **Office Action Response**: 6 months deadline
- **Opposition Period**: 30 days after publication
- **Statement of Use**: 6 months after Notice of Allowance (extendable to 3 years)

### European Union
- **Filing to Registration**: 4-6 months (if no opposition)
- **Examination**: 1-2 months
- **Opposition Period**: 3 months after publication
- **Registration**: 1 month after opposition period ends

## Common Office Action Issues

### Likely Issues to Address

1. **Descriptiveness**
   - Issue: "AI" may be considered descriptive
   - Response: Argue "AI KING" combination is suggestive/arbitrary
   - Evidence: Show unique usage in market

2. **Specimen Issues** (Class 9)
   - Issue: Specimen does not show mark on downloadable software
   - Solution: Provide proper download page screenshot or software interface

3. **Service Description Clarity** (Classes 35, 42)
   - Issue: Services too broad or unclear
   - Solution: Clarify with specific examples and narrow if needed

4. **Likelihood of Confusion**
   - Issue: Similar existing marks
   - Response: Distinguish AI KING from cited marks
   - Factors: Different goods/services, different markets, coexistence

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Conduct thorough trademark search before filing
- Use clear, specific descriptions of goods/services
- Provide high-quality specimens showing mark in use
- Respond promptly to Office Actions
- Monitor for oppositions and citations
- Use mark consistently in commerce
- Keep accurate records of first use dates

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't file before conducting a search
- Don't use generic or highly descriptive terms alone
- Don't miss Office Action deadlines
- Don't change mark after filing (file new application if needed)
- Don't abandon application without good reason
- Don't let registration lapse (maintain renewals)
